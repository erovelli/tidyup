Simple marinara sauce. Ingredients: 2 tbsp olive oil, 4 cloves garlic minced,
1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp dried oregano, fresh basil, salt and pepper.
Saute garlic in oil until fragrant. Add tomatoes and oregano, simmer 20 minutes.
Stir in basil. Serve over pasta or use as a base for baked dishes.
